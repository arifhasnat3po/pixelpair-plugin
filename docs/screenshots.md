## Brand assets

The [brand directory](../brand/) contains the mark, horizontal wordmark, monochrome variants and a size/background preview. WordPress.org icons and banners are in [.wordpress-org](../.wordpress-org/). These assets describe the project; they do not imply approval by WordPress.org or an advertising platform.

![PixelPair banner](../.wordpress-org/banner-772x250.png)

Regenerate assets with `npm run brand`. The source font and its OFL license are retained in `brand/fonts/`; the font is not loaded by the admin or included in the installation ZIP.
